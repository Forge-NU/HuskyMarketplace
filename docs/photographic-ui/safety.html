STUDENT MARKETPLACE SAFETY

Make every meetup a safer one.

Practical guidance for buying, selling and coordinating moving help.

01

Meet in public.

Arrange handoffs in well-lit, populated places. Let someone you trust know your plans. Avoid sharing your dorm room or home address in public listings.

02

Inspect before paying.

Ask about defects, missing parts and condition. Confirm the item matches its listing before exchanging payment. Husky doesn't hold funds or offer escrow.

03

Protect account information.

Never send passwords, one-time verification codes, banking credentials or payment card numbers in Messages or moving inquiries.

04

Agree on the moving job.

Before accepting a service, confirm items, stairs, timing, who supplies equipment and whether a fee is hourly or fixed. Do not take on work you cannot do safely.

05

Trust your judgment.

If a situation feels unsafe, cancel and leave. For immediate danger, call local emergency services or campus emergency assistance.

06

Report problems.

Reporting, moderation and verified accounts are not connected in this prototype. These require real backend support before a public launch.

Husky Marketplace is independently operated and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Northeastern University. Real safety reporting and dispute processes must be designed and reviewed before launch.
